ASMI (African Semiconductor Manufacturing Industries) is a Rwandan-based semiconductor manufacturing company, located at 67 Northdaam Street, Kenswatt Complex.

We deal in making HBM chips for GPU usage, utilizing the latest GDDR7 and in-scope for a possible custom GDDR-X chip (equivalent to projected speeds of GDDR8)

In addition, we manufacture consumer-oriented GPUs for gaming and creative workloads, with competitive Ray Accelerators, Rasterization Units and Multi-threaded parallel flowlanes.

Our GPUs are Nova Pro 10, with 64 Ray units, 128 ROPs and 84MB V-Cache Technology

The second is Nova Max 10 with 128 Ray Units, 170 ROPs and 104MB V-Cache

In addition, we house fabrication of custom cores, both CPUs and GPUs and collaborate with AMD, NVIDIA, Samsung and Intel.
