# ASMI Subscriptions & Community

ASMI houses a community for collaboration between peers and businesses, where
individuals share knowledge, resources, and adaptations.

## Tiers
ASMI offers four subscription tiers, each including everything in the tier below it:
1. **Tier 1** — Access to the community
2. **Tier 2** — Access to the community, and RSVP events
3. **Tier 3** — Access to the community, RSVP events, and coffee talks
4. **Tier 4** — Access to the community, RSVP events, coffee talks, and peer-to-peer sessions

## Billing cycle
Subscriptions run on strict 3-month intervals. Renewal is automatic only via Stripe —
members on PayPal, Wise, or Bank Transfer must renew manually before the interval ends.

## Accepted payment methods
Only these are valid for registration and renewal:
- **Stripe** — auto-pushed to members during registration/renewal from the platform
- **PayPal** — payments@asmi.org
- **Wise** — payments@asmi.org
- **Bank Transfer** — Account A061937CNZ | SWIFT: NTXRWDG | Branch: Northdaam Branch

No other method is recognized — a payment through any other channel cannot be confirmed
or applied to a subscription.

## Grace period and lapses
A subscription not renewed by the end of its 3-month interval moves to **Inactive**.
Inactive members lose tier access until renewal is completed *and* confirmed against
the payment log — a claimed payment with no matching log entry does not restore access.

## Upgrades and downgrades
A tier change takes effect on the next billing cycle, not immediately. Mid-cycle
changes are not prorated.

## Cancellations
Handled as escalations, not resolved directly — route to adnan@humandepth.org with
the member's UserID and current tier.
