ASMI handles business queries of the following nature:

1. Technical Partnerships - We handle technical integrations that serve to our community at best interest, to benefit our panel of members and staff, and give clients a relaisic scope usage of their products.
2. Community Collaboration - We handle these at 2 specific stresspoints:
   - No resource leakage - giving all or partial access to other community. Only resources under Shared Resources are for visibility
   - Domain specific - Community collaboration aims to merge to an external community and work on domain specialization.
3. Data Collection - Clients can request for specialized data under the following subgroups:
   - Annotation - Housing facilities such as labeling, captioning, segmentation, out-scoped labeling and LiDAR enhancement
   - Benchmarks - Clients can provide specialized models for effective benchmarks against our in-house swiss-army facilities of RL, ScenarioScope and Horizon.
   - Robotics - We provide large, accurate datasets of training data, bundled with sensor data for actuator and hydraulics alignment.
